Search by market
Browse by market and what you see (posts, marketplace, directories, events) is filtered to that area. Switch markets to see what’s there.
The feed
See what’s happening in your area, including conversations between sailors, owners, pros, and clubs via posts, forums and blogs. This is the place to share your industry knowledge and interact with potential customers.
The marketplace
Browse boats, gear, and parts for sale. Skip the noise of general marketplaces and connect directly with people who value what you post.
Where to start as a Boat Pro
 
Set up your listing
Add what you do, contact info, and the categories you work in.
Why: owners search by market and category, so this is how they find you.
 
Choose your markets
The number depends on your plan.
Why: owners in each market can find you.
 
Add your logo and photos
As your plan allows.
Why: owners judge a business by how it looks before they call.
 
Watch your inbox
Owners can message you straight from your listing.
Why: quick replies win jobs.
 
Confirm your reviews
Two-way reviews rely on your confirmation and feedback before they appear on your listing.
Why: reviews from real customers build trust before the first call.
 
Share what you know
Post a “How to” blog, respond to a forum question, and see what owners and sailors are talking about.
Why: sharing industry insights, answering questions, and engaging with your local market builds trust.
A quick note on plans
Sail More Now has free and paid options, and a few features, like reviews, direct messages and a Crew Bank listing, depend on your plan. Whichever one you’re on, the steps above are the best place to start.
