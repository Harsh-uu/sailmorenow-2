We’re so excited you’re here. Sail More Now was created so you can spend less time searching the web and more time on the water. This note walks you through where to start as a Boat Owner, and why each step helps.
Use it wherever you are
Sail More Now works on the web at app.sailmorenow.com and as an app from the Apple and Google Play stores, with one login everywhere. Whether you are managing future outings, events, or boat projects, you will have more resources available in one spot, on the spot.
Search by market
Pick your market and what you see (events, pros, clubs, posts) is filtered to where you sail. Portland/Vancouver is our home market at launch, and you’ll see events from other markets too. Heading somewhere else for a race or a trip? Switch markets to see what’s there.
The feed
See what’s happening in your market, including updates, questions, and Sailing Opportunities. New posts are how you hear about things first.
The marketplace
Browse boats, gear, and parts for sale. Skip the noise of general marketplaces and connect directly with people who value what you have.
Where to start as a Boat Owner
 
Find crew
Browse the Seasonal and Per Event Crew Banks, and post a Sailing Opportunity in the feed.
Why: sailors share their experience, personality/sailing style, and availability, so you fill the spot with someone who fits.
 
Hire with confidence
Search the boat pro directory.
Why: pros are reviewed, so you know who’s reliable before you call.
 
Plan your season
Open the events calendar.
Why: you see races and events in one place and can line up crew early.
 
Complete your profile
Why: sailors and pros can see who they’re dealing with.
 
Mark your pros as Hired
Tap Hired? on a pro you’ve used, and leave a review where your plan includes it.
Why: your feedback helps the next owner hire well.
A quick note on plans
Sail More Now has free and paid options, and a few features, like reviews, direct messages and a Crew Bank listing, depend on your plan. Whichever one you’re on, the steps above are the best place to start.
And if you ever feel stuck, our support team is ready to help. Check out the FAQs in the Help section or send us a message through Customer Support.
